# What could we do with spare AI time?

Suppose you have some AI usage left before your plan resets. Someone else has a hard problem and could use a hand. A market could offer you a small reward for a piece of that work, such as checking a proof. You would use your usual account and hand over the result. No one needs your login or a transfer of your quota.

The catch is that your “spare” usage might stop being spare. You might get an urgent task halfway through. So it helps if someone else can pick up where you left off. But in our [first model](../research/portable-checkpoints/paper.pdf), better odds of finding a substitute also tempt the buyer to offer a smaller bonus for finishing. More people quit, and the substitutes don't always make up the difference.

In one example, completion falls from **90% to about 84%**. The work still creates more value after costs, because fewer contributors have to set aside work they value highly. If the buyer can confirm whether a substitute is available before the first contributor decides to leave, and set the bonus accordingly, better backup does not reduce completion in the model.

Those numbers come from a model, not a real marketplace. The next question is what it costs to confirm a backup. You might need to pay someone to be ready, and they might never get called.
